Apa itu Expressing Opinion?

Expressing opinion adalah cara menyampaikan pendapat, sudut pandang, atau perasaan seseorang terhadap suatu hal dalam bahasa Inggris. Dalam percakapan, ungkapan ini digunakan untuk bertanya pendapat, menyampaikan pendapat, menyatakan setuju, maupun tidak setuju.

Materi disusun mengacu pada Capaian Pembelajaran Bahasa Inggris Kurikulum Merdeka (Fase E–F, Kemendikbudristek) dan referensi ungkapan komunikatif dari British Council LearnEnglish serta Cambridge English.

Asking Opinion

  • What do you think about...?
  • What's your opinion on...?
  • How do you feel about...?
  • Do you have any thoughts on...?

Giving Opinion

  • In my opinion, ...
  • I think / I believe that...
  • Personally, I feel that...
  • From my point of view, ...

Agreeing

  • I completely agree.
  • That's exactly what I think.
  • You have a point there.
  • I couldn't agree more.

Disagreeing

  • I don't think so.
  • I'm afraid I disagree.
  • I see it differently.
  • That's not quite how I see it.
